But, who am I?

My fun fact is that I'm also a fully trained tree surgeon. Just after finishing college, I found myself in a forest in the north of England cutting down trees, unsure of where life would lead me.

In hindsight, those formative years helped me realise the value of applying myself, and that further education was where I wanted to go. Since then, I taught myself A-Levels in Maths and Economics, completed a BSc in Economics at university, and spent two years working in South Korea. Whilst working in South Korea, I saw a gap in the market for a boutique-style recruitment company that better assisted teachers in getting jobs abroad.

I'm still learning about myself, and I don't think that ever fully stops, but I'm thankful for the people I've met who guided me. They taught me lessons no book ever could, and they're the kind of people I want to keep surrounding myself with: no ego, no judgement, just a genuine desire to understand and learn from my peers.
