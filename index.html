But, who am I?

My fun fact is that I'm actually a fully trained tree surgeon, which people never quite believe when I tell them. About 10 years ago, I was a bit lost and found myself in a forest in the north of England cutting down trees, having just finished school with no real idea what I wanted to do next.

Looking back, that's the most transformative experience of my life. It's what made me realise the value of applying myself and using education to get where I wanted to go. Since then, I taught myself A-Levels in Maths and Economics, studied BSc Economics at university, and spent two years living and working in South Korea.

I'm still figuring things out, and I don't think that ever fully stops, but the most important thing I've learned along the way is about people. I've met incredible people who've taught me lessons no book could, and that's what I want to bring into whatever I do next: no ego, no judgement, just a genuine desire to understand and learn from people.
